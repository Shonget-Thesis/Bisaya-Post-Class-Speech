## Self-report distribution

- Valence: 10 unpleasant, 13 neutral, 16 pleasant, and 1 very pleasant.
- Primary binary modeling outcome: 17 `Pleasant` and 23 `Not Pleasant`.
- Arousal: 3 very low, 10 low, 13 moderate, and 14 high.
- No participant has a very-high arousal rating.

The primary binary target maps ratings 4-5 to `Pleasant` and ratings 1-3 to `Not Pleasant`. This is an implementation decision made for small-sample modeling; it must be disclosed in the report and must not be described as a protocol requirement.

## Coverage limitations

- Year-level effects cannot be estimated because only first-year students are represented.
- Session effects cannot be estimated because all observations are morning recordings.
- Only three class-activity categories are represented.
- The updated metadata contains 20 observations labeled exactly `Kapoy`: 8 pleasant, 9 neutral, and 3 unpleasant. Same-word results must remain exploratory because this is still a small subset and the label normalization process should be documented.
- Device model, microphone distance, ambient-noise level, and the AM/PM time boundary are not documented in the repository.

## Feature artifact

The feature-extraction workflow defines 94 acoustic features per recording: duration; four voiced-frame F0 summaries; three RMS summaries; two zero-crossing summaries; six spectral summaries; and 78 MFCC/delta/delta-delta summaries. The generated table was checked for the expected count and finite values.

The regenerated feature artifact contains 40 rows and 109 total columns: 15 metadata/derived-target columns and 94 acoustic columns. It includes `f0_max`, `f0_min`, and `rms_max`, and its participant, spoken-word, and audio-filename fields match the current metadata.

## Execution and preliminary model check

The authoritative evaluation was generated by `export_audio_outputs.py` using five-fold participant-grouped validation. The strongest exploratory candidate in the current output package was the RBF support-vector machine:

- Mean accuracy: 0.550, compared with a 0.575 majority-class accuracy baseline.
- Mean fold macro F1: 0.522 with standard deviation 0.097.
- Mean balanced accuracy: 0.543.

The selected model did not outperform the majority-class baseline on mean accuracy. These results support continued exploratory work but do not demonstrate a validated affect-recognition system.

## Readiness decision

The dataset and code are technically ready for an exploratory group report. Publication, public sharing, or class-wide merging additionally requires documented consent, institutional approval, a shared session definition, and a common schema.
